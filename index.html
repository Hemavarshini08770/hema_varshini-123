09 / Quick answers

Clear intent.
Honest progress.

What is Dragon’s Den?

A talent, opportunity and entrepreneurship ecosystem designed to help students and aspiring founders build skills, demonstrate abilities and connect with relevant people and opportunities.

What is launching first?

The initial focus is Dragon’s Den Launchpad, centred on internships, placements and career development. Specific programmes will be announced when confirmed.

Are internships or jobs guaranteed?

No. Opportunities depend on participating organisations, eligibility, selection processes and availability.

Can an organisation partner with Dragon’s Den?

Companies, mentors, educators and community organisations can express interest in exploring a potential collaboration.

Is the member login live?

No. This is a front-end prototype. Authentication and form submissions need a secure backend before launch.
